Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Every sink, sprayer, and dish cycle draws from the tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and a high-temperature dish machine needs a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Chino Hills Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we plan service around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a core concern for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping part of the system online whenever the layout allows.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in tanks stored too cool, and costly water damage when a tank fails. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.
